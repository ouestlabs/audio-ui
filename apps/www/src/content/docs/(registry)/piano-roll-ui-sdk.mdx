The piano roll's behavior is one description, `pianoRoll` in `@audio-ui/primitives`, over the controller of `@audio-ui/core/piano-roll`. It is a grid of MIDI notes you draw, move, resize and select, with rows by pitch (folded to the notes or to a scale), a marquee, bars for the velocity or the chance to play, and the commands of an editor on the keys. Each framework renders it with a thin adapter that you copy into your project and own, and one suite of tests runs against each adapter.

The primitives are built on the [UI SDK](https://ui-sdk.dev) kernel, in beta. `@audio-ui/primitives` is not published yet: this page describes what the next release installs. The [timeline](/docs/timeline-ui-sdk) it sits on, the [piano](/docs/piano-ui-sdk) it draws its keys with and the other controls are built the same way.

## Usage

A Root holds the notes and their selection. The roll is a column of parts you place in it:

- **Grid**: the surface the notes are drawn on, a lane of the timeline. A press draws a note in draw mode, or starts a marquee in select mode; a double click adds or removes a note; Enter adds one at the playback position.
- **Rows** and **Row**: a row for each pitch drawn, to style from `data-color`, `data-in-scale`, `data-scale-root`, `data-octave` and `data-active`.
- **Notes** and **Note**: a note for each note of the roll, in reading order. A press selects it and a drag moves it. A **NoteHandle** resizes an edge.
- **Marquee**: the box a drag across the bare grid draws. **SelectionSpan** shows the stretch the selection covers, and a **StretchHandle** in it stretches the selected notes.
- **VelocityLane** and **Velocity**: a bar under each note, whose height is its velocity (or its chance, with `level="chance"`), set by a drag or through its native input.
- **Keys**: gives the pitches drawn, the ones sounding and the audition calls, to draw a vertical [piano](/docs/piano-ui-sdk) beside the grid. The piano's keys are placed by percentage of its height, so give it the height of the grid (`h-full` in a Keys part that stretches beside it), or it collapses to nothing.

```tsx
import type { Note } from "@audio-ui/primitives/piano-roll";
import { Piano } from "@/components/audio-ui/piano/react";
import { PianoRoll } from "@/components/audio-ui/piano-roll/react";

export function Roll({ notes, onNotes }: { notes: Note[]; onNotes: (notes: Note[]) => void }) {
  return (
    <PianoRoll.Root
      className="flex"
      duration={16}
      maxPitch={72}
      minPitch={48}
      mode="select"
      onValueChange={onNotes}
      snapStep={0.25}
      value={notes}
    >
      <PianoRoll.Keys className="w-16">
        {({ active, keyOff, keyOn, pitches }) => (
          <Piano.Root
            activeNotes={active}
            className="h-full"
            layout="even"
            onNoteOff={keyOff}
            onNoteOn={keyOn}
            orientation="vertical"
            pitches={pitches}
          >
            {({ pitches }) => pitches.map((pitch) => <Piano.Key key={pitch} pitch={pitch} />)}
          </Piano.Root>
        )}
      </PianoRoll.Keys>
      <PianoRoll.Grid className="relative h-64 flex-1">
        <PianoRoll.Rows>{(pitch) => <PianoRoll.Row className="data-[color=black]:bg-muted" pitch={pitch} />}</PianoRoll.Rows>
        <PianoRoll.Notes>
          {(note) => (
            <PianoRoll.Note className="rounded bg-primary data-selected:ring-2" note={note}>
              <PianoRoll.NoteHandle className="absolute inset-y-0 right-0 w-1" edge="end" note={note} />
            </PianoRoll.Note>
          )}
        </PianoRoll.Notes>
        <PianoRoll.Marquee className="border border-primary bg-primary/10" />
      </PianoRoll.Grid>
    </PianoRoll.Root>
  );
}
```

## The timeline it sits on

The roll reads a timeline's length, grid, zoom and playback position. The UI SDK has no provider to read them from, so you give them to the Root, as the values you give the [Timeline](/docs/timeline-ui-sdk):

- `duration`, `snapStep`, `snapOffset` and `snapPoints`: the length, the grid every gesture lands on unless Cmd/Ctrl is held, and what pulls a note onto it.
- `zoom`: px per unit, or unset while the roll fits its width. Control it, and give the same value to the Timeline, so the roll and the ruler stay aligned.
- `source` or `position`: the playback position, which Enter and the shortcuts that add at the playhead read. A `source` is a live value in the shape of an external store.

The Grid and the VelocityLane are lanes the Timeline's viewport finds in its DOM, so a roll inside a Timeline's Viewport scrolls and zooms with the ruler.

## Interaction

- **Draw mode**: a press draws a note, and a drag stretches it (`drawStyle="stretch"`) or paints across cells (`"paint"`). **Select mode**: a press selects, a drag across the bare grid draws the marquee, Shift or Cmd/Ctrl adds to the selection.
- **Notes**: a drag moves the selection on the grid, over rows, Alt with a drag copies it; Delete removes it, the arrows move it, Shift with an arrow by a larger step. A double click removes a note, and one on the bare grid adds one.
- **Keys of the editor**: Cmd/Ctrl with A selects all, Escape clears the selection, and the editing commands (duplicate, quantize, legato, mute, transpose) are the controller's shortcuts on the grid and on a note.
- **Velocity bars**: a press sets the velocity where the pointer is, a drag draws across the bars, and on the input the arrows move it by a step.

A disabled roll takes none of these.

## Options

Set on the Root, as props (an attribute in kebab case on the custom element, the arrays as JSON).

| Option | Type | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `value`, `defaultValue` | `Note[]` | `[]` | The notes: `{ id, pitch, start, length, velocity?, chance?, deviation?, muted? }`. Controlled, or the starting ones. |
| `selection`, `defaultSelection` | `NoteId[]` | `[]` | The ids of the notes selected. |
| `mode` | `"draw"` or `"select"` | `"select"` | What a press on the grid does. |
| `drawStyle` | `"paint"` or `"stretch"` | | How a drag in draw mode draws. |
| `minPitch`, `maxPitch` | `number` | `48`, `72` | The pitches the rows cover. |
| `folded` | `boolean` or `"scale"` | `false` | Draw only the pitches carrying notes, and with `"scale"` those in the scale too. |
| `scale` | `{ root, scale }` | | Marks the rows in a scale. |
| `minLength` | `number` | the grid step | The shortest note. |
| `activePitches` | `number[]` | | Pitches sounding from elsewhere: they light their rows and keys. |
| `getNoteLabel` | `(note) => string` | pitch name and span | Names a note for assistive technology. |
| `createNoteId` | `() => NoteId` | `"note-<n>"` | Makes the id of a note drawn, added or duplicated. |
| `disabled` | `boolean` | `false` | Takes no gesture. |
| `duration`, `snapStep`, `snapOffset`, `snapPoints`, `zoom`, `source`, `position`, `step` | | | The timeline's values, above. |
| `onValueChange`, `onValueCommitted` | `(notes, details) => void` | | The notes changed, and a gesture that changed them ended. |
| `onSelectionChange` | `(ids, details) => void` | | The selection changed. |
| `onNoteOn`, `onNoteOff` | `(pitch, details) => void` | | A key or a note auditioned a pitch. |

A Note, a NoteHandle and a Velocity take the `note` they belong to. A Velocity takes `level` (`"velocity"` or `"chance"`), and so does the VelocityLane.

## What a change reports

`details` carries:

- `reason`: what changed the notes, such as `"draw"`, `"drag"`, `"keyboard"`, `"paste"` or a command's name.
- `source`: `"mouse"`, `"touch"`, `"pen"`, `"keyboard"`, `"programmatic"` or `"agent"`.
- `event` and `cancel()`: the DOM event behind it, and the refusal. A controlled roll keeps the notes you give it.

On the custom element, the notes are the `input` and `change` events, and the selection and the keys are `selectionchange`, `noteon` and `noteoff`, with a `detail` of the value and its reason. `event.preventDefault()` refuses a change.

## States to style from

- On the Root and every part: `data-mode`, `data-editing` while a pointer edits, `data-folded`, `data-disabled`; on the Root `data-has-selection`.
- On a note and its parts: `data-selected`, `data-muted`, `data-note` for its id. A note sets `--piano-roll-velocity` and `--piano-roll-chance`, a bar `--piano-roll-level` and `--piano-roll-deviation`, for what you draw inside them.
- On a row: `data-color` (`black` or `white`), `data-in-scale`, `data-out-of-scale`, `data-scale-root`, `data-octave`, `data-active`, and `data-pitch`.

## Differences from the React piano roll of `@audio-ui/react`

The behavior and the `data-*` state attributes are the same. These differ:

- The roll is not inside a Timeline's Root: it takes the timeline's `duration`, grid, `zoom` and position as options, which you give both.
- The parts that belong to a note take the `note` itself (its id is what keys them); `Notes` and `Rows` draw one note or one row from a function in React, a scoped slot in Vue and a snippet in Svelte.
- `Keys` gives the pitches, the active ones and the audition calls to what you draw, where the React part draws the piano itself. That keeps the roll free of a piano item, and lets you draw any keys.
- On a custom element the notes are made for you, and a note keeps its place in the DOM while it is dragged, where the React ones fall into reading order when it ends.
- Every part carries a `data-slot` attribute, such as `pianoRoll-note`.
- A change reports a `source`.
