The timeline's behavior is one description, `timeline` in `@audio-ui/primitives`, over the controllers of `@audio-ui/core/timeline`. It is the surface of an arrangement: a playback position and a zoom in units of your own (seconds, beats, steps), lanes the parts are placed on, tracks, marks, ranges such as a loop, and clips that move, trim and fade. Each framework renders it with a thin adapter that you copy into your project and own, and one suite of tests runs against each adapter.

The primitives are built on the [UI SDK](https://ui-sdk.dev) kernel, in beta. `@audio-ui/primitives` is on npm, and each item below installs it with the adapter. The [knob](/docs/knob-ui-sdk), the [fader](/docs/fader-ui-sdk), the [XY pad](/docs/xypad-ui-sdk), the [meter](/docs/meter-ui-sdk), the [envelope](/docs/envelope-ui-sdk), the [pad grid](/docs/pad-ui-sdk), the [piano](/docs/piano-ui-sdk), the [sequencer](/docs/sequencer-ui-sdk), the [plot](/docs/plot-ui-sdk) and the [waveform](/docs/waveform-ui-sdk) are built the same way.

## Usage

A Root owns the position, the zoom and the grid. Everything else is a part you place in it:

- **Viewport**: the scrolling window. Cmd/Ctrl with the wheel, or a pinch, zooms around the pointer; a clip dragged near its edges scrolls it.
- **Ruler**: the lane that moves the position. A press jumps to the pointer, a drag scrubs, Shift scrubs four times finer. It holds the native `<input type="range">` that takes the keys and carries the name.
- **Lane**: a strip the parts are placed on. Its `--timeline-unit` CSS variable is the width of one unit. With `seekable`, a press on its bare area moves the position.
- **Track**, **TrackHeader**, **TrackResizeHandle**: a row with a header that stays at the left edge, and a handle that asks for a new height.
- **Playhead**: follows the position through its `--timeline-offset` variable, with no render.
- **Mark**: a point at a `value`. With `movable`, a drag or the arrows move it.
- **Range**, **RangeHandle**: a stretch laid over a lane, such as a loop.
- **Clip**, **ClipHeader**, **ClipHandle**, **ClipFade**, **ClipFadeHandle**: a stretch of a source that moves, trims and fades.
- **Navigator**, **NavigatorWindow**, **NavigatorHandle**: a bar with the stretch the viewport shows, which a drag scrolls and zooms.

## Named parts

A clip, a range, a track and a mark each hold a controller of their own, and a part that belongs to one carries its name: `clip="take-1"` on the Clip, its header, its handles and its fades. What they report goes through the Root, with the name in the details. A Mark takes its value for a name unless it has one, which is enough for a mark that does not move.

## A live position

Give the Root a `source`, a live position in the shape of an external store: `getSnapshot()` returns it and `subscribe(listener)` is called on each tick. The Playhead, the Ruler's input and a seek follow it straight in the DOM, with no render. `createSource` from `@audio-ui/core` makes one, and `useTransportClock` from the registry gives you one on the audio clock.

## Interaction

- **Ruler**: a press seeks, a drag scrubs, and the end commits. Shift with a drag selects a stretch instead when the Root has `onSelectionChange`, to loop it.
- **Clip**: a press on its body selects it and moves the position there. Dragging its header moves it on the grid unless Cmd/Ctrl is held, over other lanes too, and scrolls the viewport near its edges. A handle trims an edge, which moves where in the source the clip begins instead of stretching it; a fade handle sets a fade, and a double click or Enter steps its shape.
- **Range**: its body moves it, its handles resize it, and with `drawable` a drag across the bare lane draws a new one.
- **Track**: its resize handle asks for a height by a drag or the arrows (Down is taller); a double click gives the height up.
- **Navigator**: dragging the window scrolls, dragging an edge zooms and holds the other edge still, and a double click shows everything.
- The arrows move by `step`, Shift or Page Up and Page Down by `largeStep`, on every input.

A disabled timeline takes none of these.

## Options

Set on the Root, as props (an attribute in kebab case on the custom element).

| Option | Type | Default | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `position`, `defaultPosition` | `number` | `0` | The playback position, controlled or starting. |
| `source` | `{ getSnapshot, subscribe }` | | A live position, followed with no render. |
| `zoom`, `defaultZoom` | `number` | fits the width | px per unit. Unset, the timeline fits its width until zoomed. |
| `minZoom`, `maxZoom` | `number` | `0`, `Infinity` | The zoom's limits. |
| `duration` | `number` | `1` | The length of the timeline, in your units. |
| `snapStep`, `snapOffset` | `number` | `0` | The grid every gesture lands on unless Cmd/Ctrl is held. 0 is no grid. |
| `snapPoints` | `number[]` | | Places that pull a press, or a dragged clip or range, onto them from a few px away. |
| `step`, `largeStep` | `number` | the grid step, ten steps | The keys' steps. |
| `disabled` | `boolean` | `false` | Takes no gesture. |
| `onPositionChange`, `onPositionCommitted` | `(value, details) => void` | | A seek, and its end. |
| `onZoomChange` | `(zoom, details) => void` | | The zoom changed, already clamped. |
| `onSelectionChange`, `onSelectionCommitted` | `(range, details) => void` | | A Shift drag on the ruler, or on a seekable lane. |
| `onClipChange`, `onClipCommitted` | `(clip, details) => void` | | A clip moved, trimmed or faded. `details.clip` names it and `details.target` says what moved. |
| `onClipSelectedChange`, `onClipRemove`, `onClipDragOffsetChange` | | | A press selects a clip, Delete asks to remove it, a move drags it up or down. |
| `onRangeChange`, `onRangeCommitted` | `(range, details) => void` | | A range moved, resized or drawn, with `details.range` and `details.edge`. |
| `onTrackHeightChange`, `onTrackHeightCommitted` | `(height, details) => void` | | A track's resize handle asks for a height, `undefined` to give it up. |
| `onMarkChange`, `onMarkCommitted` | `(value, details) => void` | | A movable mark moved. |

A Clip takes `clip`, `value` or `defaultValue` (`{ start, length, offset, fadeIn, fadeOut }`, with the fade shapes), `selected`, `movable`, `minLength` and `sourceLength`. A Range takes `range`, `value`, `drawable`, `movable`, `minLength` and `minDrawnWidth`. A Track takes `track`, `height`, `minHeight`, `maxHeight`, `step`, `largeStep` and `selected`. A Lane takes `seekable`.

## What a change reports

`details` carries:

- `reason`: `"press"`, `"drag"`, `"keyboard"`, `"input-change"`, `"wheel"`, `"double-click"` or `"draw"`.
- `source`: `"mouse"`, `"touch"`, `"pen"`, `"keyboard"`, `"programmatic"` or `"agent"`.
- `event` and `cancel()`: the DOM event behind it, and the refusal. A controlled value keeps what you give it.
- The name of the clip, range, track or mark, and for a clip or a range what moved.

On the custom element, the position is the `input` and `change` events, and the rest are `zoomchange`, `selectionchange`, `clipchange`, `clipcommit`, `clipselect`, `clipremove`, `rangechange`, `rangecommit`, `trackheightchange`, `trackheightcommit`, `markchange` and `markcommit`, each with a `detail` of the value and its reasons. `event.preventDefault()` refuses a change.

## States to style from

- `data-scrubbing` on the Root while a pointer moves the position, `data-fitted` while no zoom is set, `data-disabled` on every part.
- `data-selected`, `data-dragging` and `data-fading` (`in` or `out`) on a clip and its parts, `data-edge` on a handle, `data-clip`, `data-range`, `data-track` for the name.
- A clip sets `--clip-offset`, `--clip-source-length`, `--clip-fade-in`, `--clip-fade-out`, their `-max` and `--clip-unit` for what you draw inside it, such as a waveform.
