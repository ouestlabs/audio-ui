## The audio context

A page has one `AudioContext`, shared by every hook and engine on it, made the first time something asks for it. Ask for it where you need it:

Usage and API: [`web-audio`](/docs/lib/web-audio).

## The master bus

Connect every sound of a screen into one master bus, never straight into `ctx.destination`. One fader then sets the level of everything, and a limiter at the end keeps a loud mix from clipping.

Usage and API: [`useMasterBus`](/docs/hooks/use-master-bus).
