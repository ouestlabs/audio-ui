## Usage

`repairChannels` takes channels, their sample rate and one repair of `REPAIRS`, and gives back channels of the same length with that defect taken out. Each channel is processed on its own, the source is left as it is, and a sound with no such defect comes back as it was.

```ts
import { REPAIRS, repairChannels } from "@audio-ui/engine/repair";

repairChannels(channels, 48_000, "hum60"); // mains hum and its harmonics at 60 Hz
repairChannels(channels, 48_000, "clean"); // finds what the sound carries and picks the method
```

| Repair | Takes out |
| --- | --- |
| `clean` | What the sound carries, found by the library |
| `clicks`, `crackle` | Impulses (vinyl ticks, mouth clicks, splices) and dense ones (shellac), rebuilt from the sound around them |
| `hum50`, `hum60` | Mains hum and its harmonics |
| `noise`, `speechNoise` | Steady broadband noise, and noise that changes under a voice |
| `clipping` | Peaks cut at full scale, rebuilt |
| `wind`, `plosives` | Low rumble, and the bursts of p and b |
| `breaths`, `reverb` | Breaths between phrases, and a room's late reverb |

The registry's [`sound-operations`](/docs/lib/sound-operations) names them as operations of the [Process menu](/docs/components/base/process-menu), which the audio editor bakes as steps of undo through [`useBake`](/docs/hooks/use-bake) and the clip panel runs on a clip's source.
