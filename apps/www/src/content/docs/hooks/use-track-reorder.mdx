## Usage

`useTrackReorder` moves tracks: give each track a grip, and drag the grip, or press Up or Down on it. The copy of the dragged track's header follows the pointer, the others slide aside, and the view scrolls at its edges, with no render while it moves (`sortable-drag`). The grip and the copy are `TrackGrip` and `TrackReorderGhost`, installed with it.

It is one of the parts the [Arrangement](/docs/components/base/arrangement) composes.
