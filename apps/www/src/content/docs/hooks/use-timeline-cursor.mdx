## Usage

`useTimelineCursor` is what the keys do with the cursor. `handleKey` takes M, which adds a locator, and Alt with the arrows, which jump to the next locator or clip edge, of the picked tracks or of every track. `handleArrowKey` takes the plain arrows, Home and End, for when a key reached the view rather than a control with arrows of its own: Left and Right move the cursor on the grid, Up and Down pick a track. Both say whether the key was theirs.

It is one of the parts the [Arrangement](/docs/components/base/arrangement) composes.
