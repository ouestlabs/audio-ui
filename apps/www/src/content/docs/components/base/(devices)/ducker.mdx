Turns a sound down while another one plays: a voice over music, a kick under a pad. The other sound is the key, connected to the sidechain: it is only listened to, never heard. It plays the ducker of [audio.js](https://audio.js.org). It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

## Controls

<DeviceControls device="ducker">
<Control id="thresholdDb">The level of the key above which the sound is turned down. Drag the handle on the plot across to set it.</Control>
<Control id="ratio">How much the key's level over the threshold turns the sound down: at 4:1, 4 dB of key over the threshold take 3 dB off the sound.</Control>
<Control id="kneeDb">How gradually it starts to duck around the threshold. 0 is a hard knee.</Control>
<Control id="rangeDb">The most it turns the sound down. The plot draws it as a dotted line.</Control>
<Control id="attackMs">How fast it ducks when the key comes in.</Control>
<Control id="releaseMs">How fast the sound comes back when the key stops.</Control>
</DeviceControls>

## Panel

The panel draws the gain it puts on the sound for each level of the key, once it has settled, through the package's own compression curve: the threshold, a handle on it, and the range it never goes under.

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine). Connect the key to `engine.sidechain`; see [Sidechain](/docs/devices#sidechain).

```ts
engine.reduction; // dB being taken off, read per frame
```

The browser has no ducker node. The engine plays the processor of `@audio/dynamics-ducker`'s manifest as published, in an `AudioWorklet` with two inputs, the sound and the key, fed only while it plays: off, or until the worklet is ready, the sound goes round it. With nothing on the sidechain, it follows the sound itself, as a compressor would. The threshold, the ratio, the knee and the range change as it plays. A new attack or release builds it again: at most every 50 ms, the new one first playing the last 100 ms of the sound and of the key, then taking over in a 30 ms fade.

Measured with a tone under an 80 Hz key, the sound is turned down to within 0.7 dB of the curve, at every level of the key: 7.5 dB drawn for a key at -20 dB, 6.8 dB heard.
