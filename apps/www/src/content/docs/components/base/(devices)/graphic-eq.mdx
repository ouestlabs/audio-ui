Shapes the tone with ten bands an octave apart, from 31 Hz to 16 kHz, each on a fader of its own, as a graphic equalizer has them. For bands you place and widen yourself, use the [equalizer](/docs/components/base/equalizer). It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

Each band boosts or cuts 15 dB. A band at 0 dB leaves its octave as it is.

## Panel

The panel draws the curve the bands play together over a fader for each, as on a graphic equalizer, with the output knob beside them. `graphicEqCurve(state)` is the curve it draws, and `graphicEqBands(state)` the filters a state is.

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine).

The bands are those of `@audio/eq-graphic`: a bell at each octave centre of ISO 266, all as wide (a Q of 1.4), so they meet without a gap or a hump. Its bells are the Audio EQ Cookbook's, which a `BiquadFilterNode` of type `peaking` computes, so the engine is ten of those in series, then an output level. Off, every band goes flat and the level to 0 dB.
