A tonewheel organ. Every key sounds nine sines at the organ's harmonics, from the 16' an octave under the key to the 1' three octaves over it, each at the level its drawbar is pulled to. A percussion on the second or third harmonic and a tick of key click open each note, and the sound goes through a drive and a rotary speaker, whose horns take a moment to reach a new speed. Start from one of its six registrations (Jazz, Gospel, Rock, Church, Flute, Full), then pull the drawbars. It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

## Panel

The panel loads a registration from the menu in its title bar, beside the rotary speaker's switch, draws the nine drawbars as faders, the percussion, the key click, the drive and the volume beside them, and has a keyboard of two octaves that plays the organ. `organPreset(name)` gives a registration's whole state, and `ORGAN_PRESETS` lists them. With `activeNotes`, the notes held now, such as on a MIDI keyboard, light up their keys. The panel sends notes out and does not make the sound, so it plays through any engine.

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine).

```ts
engine.noteOn({ frequency: 261.63 }); // a key now
engine.noteOff(); // stops it

// Scheduled ahead on the audio clock, by a host that plays notes from a song:
engine.noteOn({ frequency: 440 }, { at: time, id: "a4" });
engine.noteOff({ at: time + length, id: "a4" });
```

An engine plays one key, through its own drive and rotary speaker. For chords, give each note an engine of its own: [`usePolyphony`](/docs/guides/synthesis#polyphony) keeps eight for the block above, and the [arrangement](/docs/components/base/arrangement)'s engine does it for a MIDI track. A key sounds at once and stops at once, as an organ's does, with no click. A note while one is held cuts it and sounds anew, and a `noteOff` whose `id` is not the held note's lets nothing go. A drawbar moved sounds on a held key, and a registration with few drawbars out is lifted part of the way so it is not far quieter than a full one. An agent plays the organ with the `play` tool.
