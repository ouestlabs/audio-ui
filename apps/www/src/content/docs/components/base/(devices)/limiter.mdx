Keeps the sound under a ceiling. The input gain pushes the level against it, and what goes over is held down. It is the lookahead brickwall limiter of [audio.js](https://audio.js.org), which can hold true peaks. It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

## Controls

<DeviceControls device="limiter">
<Control id="gainDb">Pushes the level of the input against the ceiling.</Control>
<Control id="ceilingDb">The level the output should not go over.</Control>
<Control id="releaseMs">How fast the gain comes back after a peak.</Control>
<Control id="lookaheadMs">How far ahead the limiter sees a peak coming: how long it takes to bring the gain down into it, and how late the sound comes out.</Control>
<Control id="truePeak">Keeps the waveform rebuilt between the samples under the ceiling too, not only the samples. It adds 96 samples of latency.</Control>
</DeviceControls>

## Panel

The panel draws the transfer curve against the straight line of a clean sound. Drag the handle up and down for the ceiling and across for the input gain.

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine).

```ts
engine.reduction; // dB being taken off, read per frame
```

The limiter is the processor of `@audio/dynamics-limiter`'s manifest as published, in an `AudioWorklet`. Until the worklet is ready, the signal passes at the input gain. Every one of its values builds it again: at most every 50 ms, the new one first playing the last 100 ms of sound, then taking over in a 30 ms fade. `engine.reduction` reads the gain it takes off, measured from its output against its input as late.

Its latency is the look-ahead, plus 96 samples with True peak on, and the device declares it, so a host that compensates latency keeps it in time. Measured on noise pushed 20 dB over the ceiling:

| True peak | Samples | Between the samples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | at the ceiling | up to 1.5 dB over it |
| On | at the ceiling | at it, read at 8 points a sample; 0.02 dB over, read at the 4 of BS.1770 |
