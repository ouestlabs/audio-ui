Keeps the sound under a ceiling. The input gain pushes the level against it, and what goes over is held down. Its model is the browser's compressor with a clip, or the lookahead brickwall limiter of [audio.js](https://audio.js.org), which can hold true peaks. It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine).

```ts
engine.reduction; // dB being taken off, read per frame
```

### Clean

The clean model is the browser's compressor node at a ratio of 20 with no knee, then a hard clip. The node adds a make-up gain of its own that cannot be turned off, so the engine takes it back out, and a signal under the ceiling is only as loud as the gain makes it.

A ratio of 20 is not a wall. Measured in Chrome, with a ceiling of -1 dB and +24 dB in, the node alone reaches +0.6 dBFS. The final clip, oversampled 4x, keeps the samples at the ceiling: in the conformance page the peak is never more than 0.05 dB over it, for ceilings of -1, -6 and -12 dB and up to +36 dB in. The filter of the oversampling rings a little, so it is not exactly zero.

That is a sample peak, not an inter-sample (true) peak. It is not a substitute for a true-peak limiter on a master that is checked against a loudness standard: use the look-ahead model with True peak on.

### Look-ahead

The look-ahead model is the processor of `@audio/dynamics-limiter`'s manifest as published, in an `AudioWorklet` fed only while it plays. Until the worklet is ready, the clean model plays for it. Every one of its values builds it again: at most every 50 ms, the new one first playing the last 100 ms of sound, then taking over in a 30 ms fade. `engine.reduction` reads the gain it takes off, measured from its output against its input as late.

Its latency is the look-ahead, plus 96 samples with True peak on, and the device declares it, so a host that compensates latency keeps it in time. Measured on noise pushed 20 dB over the ceiling:

| True peak | Samples | Between the samples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Off | at the ceiling | up to 1.5 dB over it |
| On | at the ceiling | at it, read at 8 points a sample; 0.02 dB over, read at the 4 of BS.1770 |
