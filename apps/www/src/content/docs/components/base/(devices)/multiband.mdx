Compresses three bands apart. The signal is split at two frequencies into low, mid and high, and each band has its own threshold and ratio. A loud bass does not pump the highs, and a harsh band can be held down without dulling the rest. It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

<Control id="midThresholdDb">The level above which the mid band is compressed.</Control>

<Control id="highThresholdDb">The level above which the high band is compressed.</Control>
<Control id="highRatio">How much the high band is brought down above its threshold.</Control>
<Control id="attackMs">How fast a band is taken down once it is over its threshold. The three bands share it.</Control>
<Control id="releaseMs">How fast a band comes back once it is under its threshold. The three bands share it.</Control>
<Control id="outputDb">The level of the three bands added back together.</Control>

## Panel

The panel draws the three bands against frequency, with a handle on each split to drag across. The splits have no knob. The two ranges do not overlap (40 to 1000 Hz and 1.2 to 12 kHz), so the splits can never cross. `multibandBands(state)` is what the panel draws: what each band keeps of the signal, in dB at each frequency.

```tsx
import { MultibandPanel } from "@/components/audio-ui/devices/multiband/panel";
import { multibandDevice } from "@/components/audio-ui/devices/multiband/device";

const [state, setState] = useState(() => multibandDevice.defaults());

<MultibandPanel onValueChange={setState} value={state} />;
```

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine).

```ts
engine.reductions; // { low, mid, high }: dB taken off each band, read per frame
```

Two biquads in a row make one Linkwitz-Riley filter, whose halves add back to a flat response. The low band also goes through the two halves of the high split, added, so that it is shifted in phase as the other two are and the three add back flat. Each band has its own detector, the one of the compressor's [sidechain](/docs/devices#sidechain), with a soft knee of 6 dB.

Measured in Chrome with all ratios at 1, the output is within 0.01 dB of the input from 60 Hz to 9 kHz. With a 100 Hz tone at -6 dB over a 6 kHz tone, a threshold of -30 dB at 8:1 on the low band takes 16.4 dB off the bass and nothing off the highs. The same on the high band takes 10.6 dB off the highs and nothing off the bass.
