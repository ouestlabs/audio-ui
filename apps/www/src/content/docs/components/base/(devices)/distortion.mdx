Bends the sound into one of four curves. The more the drive, the more the peaks round off, or fold back. It is a [device](/docs/devices): a state, a panel, an engine and tools, each its own file.

## Controls

<DeviceControls device="distortion">
<Control id="curve">The shape the drive pushes the signal into, picked with a row of buttons, each drawn from its curve. Tanh, the default, rounds the peaks off gently. Cubic rounds them off sooner and holds them flat at full scale; a quiet input comes out half as loud again. Hard holds the peaks flat at full scale, with a corner. Fold reflects whatever passes full scale back inside it, again and again as the drive grows.</Control>
<Control id="driveDb">How hard the signal is pushed into the curve.</Control>
<Control id="toneHz">Closes the highs after the curve.</Control>
<Control id="mix">The balance of the distorted signal and the clean one.</Control>
<Control id="outputDb">The level of the output.</Control>
</DeviceControls>

## Panel

The panel draws the transfer curve, the output for each input, against the straight line of a clean sound, and picks the curve with buttons drawn from each. `distortionShape(curve, input)` is a curve at an input after the drive, and `distortionCurve(state)` is the whole curve with the mix and the output.

## Engine

Create it, connect it and update it as for any device: see [The engine](/docs/devices#the-engine).

The drive is a gain before the table of the curve, so turning it never changes the table; only picking another curve does. The tables are written by the curves of `@audio/effect-distortion` (`distortionTable(curve)`) over the whole range of the drive, so a fold keeps folding at full drive. The shaper oversamples 4x to keep the curve from aliasing, which is the [latency](/docs/devices#latency) of the device.
