## Usage

`ProcessMenuItems` lists the operations that change a sound's samples, in four groups: Structure (Reverse, Repeat, Add silence), Level and tone (Normalize, Pan, Dither, Filter), Time and pitch (Stretch, Pitch, Speed, each by the method picked) and Repair. Place them in a dropdown menu's content, or a submenu's, with a trigger of your own. The audio editor's Process menu and the clip panel's are made of them.

```tsx
import { ProcessMenuItems } from "@/components/audio-ui/process-menu";
import { applySoundOperation, soundOperationName } from "@/lib/sound-operations";

<DropdownMenu>
  <DropdownMenuTrigger render={<Button aria-label="Process" size="icon" variant="outline" />}>
    <WandSparklesIcon />
  </DropdownMenuTrigger>
  <DropdownMenuContent align="start" className="w-64">
    <ProcessMenuItems
      disabled={!sound}
      onProcess={(operation) => {
        setSound(applySoundOperation(sound, operation));
        setStatus(soundOperationName(operation)); // "Repeat ×2", "Pitch -5 st"
      }}
    />
  </DropdownMenuContent>
</DropdownMenu>;
```

`onProcess` gets the operation picked, as data (`{ type: "repeat", times: 2 }`), so the menu does no audio work itself: [`sound-operations`](/docs/lib/sound-operations) runs it on plain channels and names it as a step of the history, and you decide what it runs on and when. The audio editor bakes it on the sound as edited with [`useBake`](/docs/hooks/use-bake); the [clip panel](/docs/components/base/arrangement#the-clip-panel) offers it in the bar over the clip's sound and in its right-click menu, and hands it to its host with the stretch selected, which processes the clip's source into a recording of its own.

### Items of your own

`children` go first in the Structure group, beside the operations. The audio editor puts its edits that clean a sound up there:

```tsx
<ProcessMenuItems disabled={!sound} onProcess={baking.process}>
  <DropdownMenuItem onClick={edits.trimSilence}>Trim silence</DropdownMenuItem>
  <DropdownMenuSub>
    <DropdownMenuSubTrigger>Shrink pauses</DropdownMenuSubTrigger>
    <DropdownMenuSubContent>…</DropdownMenuSubContent>
  </DropdownMenuSub>
</ProcessMenuItems>
```
