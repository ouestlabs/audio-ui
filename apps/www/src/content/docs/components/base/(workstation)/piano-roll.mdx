A piano roll for writing a phrase: a keyboard down the side, notes on a grid, and a velocity bar under each note. It sits inside a [Timeline](/docs/components/base/arrangement), so notes use the timeline's units, snap to its grid and follow its zoom.

## Overview

- **Notes:** press on the grid to draw a note or to start a selection rectangle, depending on the mode. Drag a note to move it, drag an edge to resize it, and double-click to delete it. Selected notes move and resize together.
- **Keys:** pressing a key on the side plays its note. The rows light up for the pitches playing.
- **Velocity:** each note has a bar in its own lane, drawn from the bottom.
- **Keyboard:** every edit works without a pointer, including copy, paste, duplicate and quantize.
- **Accessibility:** each note is a toggle button named after its pitch and span. Each velocity bar holds a native `<input type="range">`.

## Usage

```tsx
import { Timeline, TimelineNavigator } from "@/components/audio-ui/timeline";
import {
  PianoRoll,
  PianoRollGrid,
  PianoRollKeys,
  PianoRollStrip,
  PianoRollVelocity,
  PianoRollVelocityScale,
  PianoRollViewport,
} from "@/components/audio-ui/piano-roll";

<Timeline duration={16} snapStep={1}>
  <PianoRoll
    className="h-96"
    maxPitch={72}
    minPitch={48}
    onNoteOn={(pitch, details) => play(pitch, details.velocity)}
    onValueChange={setNotes}
    value={notes}
  >
    <PianoRollViewport>
      <PianoRollStrip className="flex-1" lead={<PianoRollKeys />}>
        <PianoRollGrid aria-label="Notes" gridStep={1} />
      </PianoRollStrip>
      <PianoRollStrip lead={<PianoRollVelocityScale />} sticky="bottom">
        <PianoRollVelocity gridStep={1} />
      </PianoRollStrip>
    </PianoRollViewport>
  </PianoRoll>
</Timeline>;
```

A note is `{ id, pitch, start, length, velocity?, deviation?, muted?, chance? }`: `pitch` is a MIDI number (60 is middle C), `start` and `length` are in the timeline's units, `velocity` is 0 to 1 (full when left out), `deviation` is how far the velocity may stray each time it plays (-1 to 1, up or down), `muted` deactivates it (it stays on the roll, silent) and `chance` is how likely it is to play each time round, 0 to 1. The roll draws them; playing them is yours, so a sequencer skips a muted note, rolls the dice for its chance and strays its velocity by up to its deviation. `value` and `selection` are controlled; `defaultValue` and `defaultSelection` leave them to the roll. The roll reports every edit through `onValueChange` and, when a gesture or a key press ends, `onValueCommitted`; `eventDetails.edit` tells what happened (`"add"`, `"move"`, `"resize"`, `"remove"`, `"duplicate"`, `"paste"`, `"quantize"`, `"stretch"`, `"velocity"`, `"chance"` or `"deviation"`) and `eventDetails.cancel()` keeps the notes as they were.

`onNoteOn` and `onNoteOff` say when a pitch should sound or stop: a key pressed, a note drawn, picked up or moved to a new pitch.

`scale` marks the rows of a scale (`data-in-scale` on each `PianoRoll.Row` in it, `data-out-of-scale` on the others, and `data-scale-root` on its root's): the styles leave the scale's rows clear, shade the others in place of the black keys, and edge the root's in the primary color, and `folded="scale"` keeps only those rows and the ones carrying notes. The roll takes a scale as its pitch classes and its root; `@audio-ui/engine/pitch` holds the scales by name (`SCALES`, `inScale`, `nearestInScale`) and `rollScale`, which gives one in that shape, and `@audio-ui/core/piano-roll` the edits a toolbar or a menu applies to the selection, each returning the new notes: `splitNotes`, `chopNotes`, `joinNotes`, `legatoNotes`, `reverseNotes`, `invertNotes`, `stretchNotes`, `fitNotesToScale`, `rampVelocity`, `humanizeNotes`, `toggleMuted`, `setChance`, `setDeviation` and `changeLevel`, and the time commands `deleteTime`, `insertTime` and `duplicateTime`; `selectionSpan` gives the time a selection covers and `findNotes` the notes a query matches, by pitch, velocity, length, chance, scale, state or count.

The toolbar of the preview above is three menus that install on their own: the [Scale menu](/docs/components/base/scale-menu) picks the scale the roll marks, [Note find](/docs/components/base/note-find) selects notes by what they are, and the [Note edit menu](/docs/components/base/note-edit-menu) runs the commands above on the selection.

### MusicXML files

The `musicxml-file` library does the same for a score, the format notation apps write: it reads a `.musicxml` or `.xml` file, or one compressed as `.mxl`, onto the roll's steps and writes one back. `npx shadcn@latest add @audio-ui/musicxml-file`.

```tsx
import {
  downloadMusicXmlFile,
  MUSICXML_FILE_ACCEPT,
  readMusicXmlFile,
} from "@audio-ui/engine/files";

const { bpm, notes, title } = await readMusicXmlFile(file, { duration: 64 });

<Button onClick={() => downloadMusicXmlFile(notes, { bpm, filename: "clip.musicxml" })}>
  Export as MusicXML
</Button>
```

`readMusicXmlFile` takes the grid `readMidiFile` takes and returns the score's first tempo and its title. It reads every part into the one clip, in the beats they were written in, through changes of tempo: chords, ties across the bar line, a second voice. It drops the grace notes, which take no time, plays the repeats once, as written, and reads a transposing instrument as written, not as it sounds. The score is parsed by `@audio/musicxml`. `writeMusicXmlFile` writes the notes on their grid, so a notation app shows them where they are. `readMusicXmlFileParts(file)` reads a score as parts, as `readMidiFileParts` does a MIDI file. A compressed score is opened with `fflate`.

### Driving it from an agent

`createPianoRollTools` gives a language model the tools to write on the roll from what a user asks ("a walking bassline in C minor", "up an octave"): `setNotes`, `addNotes`, `removeNotes`, `transpose`, `clear` and `setTempo`, each a description, a zod schema and an `apply` that returns the next state. `npx shadcn@latest add @audio-ui/piano-roll-tools`.

```tsx
import { tool } from "ai";
import { createPianoRollTools } from "@/lib/piano-roll-tools";

const roll = createPianoRollTools({ maxPitch: 72, minPitch: 48, stepCount: 16 });

const tools = Object.fromEntries(
  Object.entries(roll).map(([name, t]) => [
    name,
    tool({
      description: t.description,
      execute: (args) => {
        setState((state) => t.apply(state, args as never));
        return `applied ${name}`;
      },
      inputSchema: t.schema,
    }),
  ])
);
```

The pitch range and the grid are in the schemas, and every note a model writes is clamped into them, so whatever it answers lands on the roll's rows and steps. The tools read and write `{ notes, bpm }` and depend on no agent library: the example wires them to the AI SDK, and any tool-calling API takes the same description and schema.

## Interaction

### Pointer

<KeyTable title="Gestures">
<KeyRow gesture={"Press on empty grid"}>With `mode="draw"`, draws a note whose end follows the pointer. With `mode="select"`, starts a selection rectangle (a double-click draws). `Shift` adds to the selection.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Drag a note"}>Moves it, with the selection when it is part of it. On the grid unless <kbd>Cmd</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> is held.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Alt` + drag a note"}>Moves a copy.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Drag a note's edge"}>Resizes it: the end changes the length, the start trims it from the front.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Shift` + click a note"}>Toggles it in the selection.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Double-click a note"}>Deletes it.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Drag a velocity bar"}>Changes the velocity of the selection by the same amount.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + drag a velocity bar"}>Sets how far the velocity of the selection may stray each time it plays.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Drag across a velocity or chance lane"}>With `mode="draw"`, paints every bar under the pointer to its height.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Drag a stretch handle"}>Stretches the selection in time from its other edge.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Press a key"}>Plays it; with `Shift`, selects every note on its row.</KeyRow>
</KeyTable>

### Keyboard

<KeyTable title="Keys">
<KeyRow gesture={"Arrow keys on a note"}>`Left` / `Right` move it by a grid step, `Up` / `Down` by a semitone. With `Shift`: resize it, or move an octave.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Enter`, `Space` on a note"}>Toggle it in the selection.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Delete`, `Backspace`"}>Remove the selected notes.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Enter` on the grid"}>Adds a note at the playback position.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Escape`, `Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `A`"}>Clear the selection, select every note.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `D`"}>Repeat the selection right after itself and select the copies.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Cmd` / `Ctrl` + `C`, `X`, `V`"}>Copy, cut, and paste at the playback position.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"`Q`"}>Snap the starts of the selection (every note, with none selected) to the grid.</KeyRow>
<KeyRow gesture={"Arrow keys, `Page Up` / `Down`, `Home` / `End` on a velocity bar"}>Change the velocity by 5%, by 25%, or to silent or full.</KeyRow>
</KeyTable>

### Registry components

`PianoRoll` takes the `Root` props. The rest lay the editor out on two columns, the keys' and the timeline's:

<TypeTable title="Components">
<TypeRow name={"PianoRollViewport"}>Scrolls the roll both ways, without scrollbars: put a `TimelineNavigator` above it to scroll and zoom across.</TypeRow>
<TypeRow name={"PianoRollStrip"}>A row of the editor: `lead` in the keys' column, stuck to the left edge, the lanes in `children` beside it. `sticky` keeps it at the `"top"` or `"bottom"` of the viewport.</TypeRow>
<TypeRow name={"PianoRollKeys"}>The keyboard down the side, one key per row. `size` is `"sm"`, `"default"` or `"lg"`; `names`, by pitch, writes a name on a key in place of its note's, such as a drum rack's pads.</TypeRow>
<TypeRow name={"PianoRollGrid"}>The notes, on their rows. `gridStep` draws a line every step, `accentStep` a firmer one, such as each beat; anything placed on the timeline, such as a `TimelinePlayhead`, goes in `children`.</TypeRow>
<TypeRow name={"PianoRollVelocity"}>Each note's velocity as a bar.</TypeRow>
<TypeRow name={"PianoRollVelocityScale"}>The scale beside the velocity lane, for the `lead` of its strip.</TypeRow>
</TypeTable>
