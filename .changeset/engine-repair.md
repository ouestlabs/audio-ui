Repair: a new `@audio-ui/engine/repair` module whose `repairChannels` takes a defect out of a recording, by the method made for it: clicks, crackle, mains hum at 50 or 60 Hz, steady and changing noise, clipping, wind, plosives, breaths and reverb, or `clean`, which finds what the sound carries. It is built on `@audio/denoise`.
