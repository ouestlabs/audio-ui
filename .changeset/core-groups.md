Playback: tracks can play into group tracks. `routedCompensation(tracks, returns)` lines every path up wherever paths meet, through groups within groups and sends at any depth, with a wait for each track's way out and for each send. A `SoloTrack` can name its `group`: a soloed track keeps the groups it is in heard, and a soloed group lets the tracks in it be heard.
