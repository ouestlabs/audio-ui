Timeline has tracks: `Timeline.Track` is a row with a header beside a lane and a height you hold (`height`, `onHeightChange`, `onHeightCommitted`, `selected`), `Timeline.TrackHeader` is the strip that names it and stays at the left edge while the lane scrolls, and `Timeline.TrackResizeHandle` resizes it by dragging or with the arrow, Page and Home/End keys through a native range input.
