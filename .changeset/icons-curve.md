Icons: `curveGlyph` and `CurveIcon` draw a function. `curveGlyph(fn, { from, to, min, max })` returns the glyph of a curve on the 24 grid, with the margin of the other icons; `CurveIcon` (from `@audio-ui/icons/react`) renders it with a `weight` and a `size`, so a button can draw what a device's own function does, in the style of the rest of the set.
